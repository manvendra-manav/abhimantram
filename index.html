Why the name

Abhimantramअभिमन्त्रम्

From Sanskrit, the act of charging something with a mantra, so that it carries intention.

We believe a piece of jewellery should do more than shine. It is worn at the moment someone gets married, lights a lamp at Diwali, or walks into a first job interview. Abhimantram designs for those moments.

Our imitation jewellery borrows its shapes from temple, kundan and traditional Indian designs, and is finished in warm gold with touches of saffron and ivory. It gives you the look of fine jewellery at a price that lets you own more than one set.

Every piece is made by hand and blessed with the mantra of beauty. Every collection carries a Sanskrit name, because each design starts from a word and the idea behind it. We sell through Meesho, so our pieces reach customers across India directly, without a showroom markup.

How to keep it shining

Imitation jewellery lasts longer with a little care. Four habits make the difference.

  1. Put it on lastApply perfume, hairspray and lotion first, then wear your jewellery.
  2. Keep it dryRemove before bathing, swimming or workouts, and wipe off sweat with a soft cloth.
  3. Store it apartKeep each piece in its pouch or box, away from damp and from other pieces.
  4. Wipe gentlyUse a dry, soft cloth. Avoid rubbing hard on stones and kundan work.
